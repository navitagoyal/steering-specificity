This is the official repository of our paper **Steering Safely or Off a Cliff? Rethinking Specificity and Robustness in Inference-Time Interventions**. 

This codebase includes an evaluation framework that assesses whether steering methods alter *only* the intended property. 
The specificity is evaluated along three dimensions: general (preserving fluency and unrelated abilities), control (preserving related control properties), and robustness (preserving control properties under distribution shifts).
The current codebase supports evaluation in the case of overrefusal steering and faithfulness hallucination steering, but can be extended to other datasets with ease.
